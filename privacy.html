Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 9, 2026

HR Outreach Bot is a tool that helps users send personalized recruiter outreach emails through their own Gmail account.

Information We Access

When you sign in with Google, the application may access your Google account email address and Gmail permission required to send emails on your behalf.

How We Use Information

Information accessed through Google is used only to provide the application's email-sending functionality and to identify your session.

Gmail Data

The application uses the Gmail API only for the functionality authorized by the user. We do not sell, rent, or use Gmail data for advertising.

Uploaded Files

Files uploaded by users, such as recruiter contact lists and resumes, are used only to perform the requested outreach operation.

Data Sharing

We do not sell or share users' personal information with third parties except where required to operate the service or comply with applicable law.

Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information used by the application. Users should only upload information they are authorized to use.

Contact

For questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact the application developer through the contact information provided on the application's Google OAuth consent screen.
